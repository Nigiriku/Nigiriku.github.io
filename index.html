About Me

Halo! I am Lee Ah Kim, born and raised in Bandar Melaka.

Growing up right near the Malacca Strait, history is not just something inside dusty textbooks to me, it is right on our doorstep. Every shophouse tile, old riverbank wall, and local dialect here has a story about how foreign traders, colonial powers, and regional empires collided and lived together.

At the same time, I have always been fascinated by modern code. I picked up Python properly to bridge these two worlds. But as much as I love tech and computational methods, I am very cautious about where computing is heading today, especially with modern Large Language Models and heavy AI infrastructure. Training massive models consumes huge amounts of energy, water, and compute power, often at the expense of our environment.

For me, programming and AI should be used smartly, ethically, and sustainably. We should not burn up precious planetary resources just to run inefficient algorithms. My long term academic goal is to work at the intersection of Computational History, Sustainable AI, and Digital Humanities, using lean, green data structures and lightweight models to analyze and preserve history without destroying our future.
